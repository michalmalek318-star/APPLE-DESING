The gap is simple: a buyer in another time zone writes while your office sleeps.

Agencies and developers pay for portals, campaigns and overseas fairs. The most expensive moment, the first reply, still depends on whether someone happens to be awake and speaks the language.

Hazeo Engine closes that gap and adds the other half: active outreach to partner agencies and investors in the countries your buyers come from.

The niche

A cross-border deal desk, run by an AI agent.

Not another website chatbot. Hazeo runs the two streams that today need an expensive international sales team: transactions with foreign buyers, and capital for investment projects.

For agencies whose buyers live somewhere else

Agencies with 10–150 brokers in places the world buys into: resort coasts, capitals, residency-by-investment markets.

  • 24/7 handling of portal, WhatsApp and website enquiries in the buyer's language
  • Qualification and lead scoring written straight into your CRM
  • Partner network: the agent contacts agencies in your buyers' home countries and sets up referral terms
  • Matching across off-plan and resale, booking video viewings

Good fit if

FAQ

Common questions

Does the AI agent replace my brokers?

No. It takes the first reply, qualification, scheduling and reminders off their plate. People run the conversation, the negotiation and the signature.

How long does setup take?

The audit takes 10 working days. The agent answers enquiries roughly 2–3 weeks after kickoff, once your CRM, listings and channels are connected.

Which languages does it speak?

Six at launch: English, German, Polish, Spanish, French and Arabic. More on request, always checked by a native speaker first.

Do you guarantee sales?

No. We guarantee response time, qualification quality and reporting. Sales depend on the inventory, the price and your team.

Does it work in my market?

We start where foreign demand is highest: UAE, Spain, Portugal, Cyprus, Greece and Poland. We check local brokerage rules before going live in any country.
